Good to know

  • One click to switch a site off. Click a site's switch in the toolbar popup to stop blocking it straight away, and click it again to turn blocking back on. Open tabs follow at once, with no reload.
  • Allowed times. Under each site in the popup, Allowed times lets it through at set times, like YouTube from 8 to 9pm on weekdays. Adding or lengthening a time takes a 30-second wait; shortening or removing one is instant.
  • Focus sessions. When one click is too easy, start a focus session at the top of the popup: every site is blocked for 30 minutes to 2 hours, and the switches stay locked until it ends. Or press Alt+Shift+F twice to start an hour without opening the popup.
  • The popup keeps count of what ShortStop blocked today.
  • Nothing leaves your device. No account, no analytics, no network requests.
